The prompt names no brand. Find the look and the accent the design settles on (its Brief or Assumed line). The plugin has three named looks, "the house look", "Ledger" and "Boutique", and eight accents, teal, cyan, blue, indigo, violet, purple, plum and rose. PASS when the accent named is one of those eight words and the look, when one is named, is one of those three. A description of the named look in a few words ("Boutique, in plum: warm paper and a serif for headings") or a reason for the choice ("like the paper's own pages", "Ledger, since the editors work the queue all day") is fine and does not fail it: Boutique is warm paper and a serif, Ledger is grey and compact, the house look is cool paper and a grotesque. FAIL when the accent named is not one of the eight words (a hex value, "ink", "black", a brand), when a look is named that is not one of the three, when the design invents a look of its own in place of the three (a theme such as "newsprint" named as the look itself, a typeface by name that is not the look's, its own hex tones), or when the design says nothing about the accent. Quote the sentence you relied on.
