The prompt names no brand. Find the accent the design settles on (its Brief or Assumed line). The plugin has one house look and nine accents: navy, teal, cyan, blue, indigo, violet, purple, plum and rose. PASS when the accent named is one of those nine words and the design changes nothing else of the look. A reason given for the choice ("the blue-black of printer's ink", "suits a masthead") is fine and does not fail it. FAIL when the accent named is not one of the nine words (a hex value, "newsprint", "ink", "black", a brand), when the design invents a look of its own (its own paper, ink or typefaces, a theme such as "newsprint" named as the look, a named look such as "Ledger" or "Boutique"), or when the design says nothing about the accent. Quote the sentence you relied on.
