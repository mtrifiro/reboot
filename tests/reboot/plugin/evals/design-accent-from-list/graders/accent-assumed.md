The prompt names no brand. Find the look and the accent the design settles on (its Brief or Assumed line). PASS if the accent's name is one of: teal, cyan, blue, indigo, violet, purple, plum, rose, and the look, when one is named, is one of: the house look, Ledger, Boutique, and the design changes nothing else of the look (no paper, ink or typeface of its own). A reason given for the choice ("the blue-black of printer's ink", "Ledger, since the editors work the queue all day") is fine and does not fail it. FAIL if the accent named is not one of those eight words (a hex value, "newsprint", "ink", "black", "warm paper", a brand), if the look named is not one of those three, if the design describes its own paper, ink or typefaces outside them, or if it names a theme as the look itself rather than as the reason for a choice. Quote the sentence you relied on; if the design says nothing about the accent, FAIL.
