The prompt names no brand. Find the accent the design settles on (its Brief or Assumed line). PASS if the accent's name is one of: teal, cyan, blue, indigo, violet, purple, plum, rose, and the design changes nothing else of the look (its paper, ink or typefaces). A reason given for the choice ("the blue-black of printer's ink", "suits a masthead") is fine and does not fail it. FAIL if the accent named is not one of those eight words (a hex value, "newsprint", "ink", "black", "warm paper", a brand), if the design says the paper, ink or typefaces change, or if it names a theme as the look itself rather than as the reason for an accent. Quote the sentence you relied on; if the design says nothing about the accent, FAIL.
